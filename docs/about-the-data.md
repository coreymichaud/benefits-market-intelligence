# Form 5500 Data

The Form 5500 Series is the annual return/report used by employee benefit plans to satisfy filing requirements under the Employee Retirement Income Security Act (ERISA) and related Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation (PBGC) oversight.
Plan sponsors or administrators file electronically through EFAST2, the government's electronic filing system, and most filings become part of the public record.

At it's core, each Form 5500 filing identifies who is sponsoring a benefit plan, what kind of plan it is, who the insurance carrier and broker are, what the plan is paying in premiums
and compensation, and how many people are covered. When assembled across employers, years, benefit lines, industries, and geographies, these filings can reveal which brokers and carriers dominate specific market segments,
how disclosed premiums, commissions, and feeds trend over time, and where compensation patterns or broker-carrier relationships look unusual relative to peers. The data supports the construction of broker
leaderboards, carrier market share views, employer-level plan economics, geographic and sector heat maps, and multi-year switching and growth analyses.

The Form 5500 data used for this analysis was gathered from the [DOL EFAST's Form 5500 Datasets](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ebsa/about-ebsa/our-activities/public-disclosure/foia/form-5500-datasets). The data includes the years of 2019-2024, all with the latest versions
of their respective forms.

## Form 5500 Main (Annual Return/Report)

The Form 5500 (Main) is the annual report that most retirement and welfare benefit plans covered by ERISA must file each year. It captures basic plan-level information such as:

- Plan name/number
- Sponsor
- Administrative details
- Plan type
- Number of participants
- Financial summary data

## Form 5500 Schedule A (Insurance Information)

The Form 5500 Schedule A is filed when a plan buys benefits through an insurance company. It discloses information such as:

- Insurer
- Contract details
- Premiums paid
- Commissions or fees to brokers/agents

## Form 5500 Schedule C Part 1, Item 2 (Service Provider Compensation)

The Form 5500 Schedule C Part 1, Item 2 is required for large plans (100+ participants) and list service providers who were paid more than $5,000 during the plan year. It documents:

- Direct and indirect compensation details
- Services performed
- Provider's relationshiop to the plan
- Compensation amounts

## Other Forms Not Included

What is not included in this analysis are the others Form 5500 forms such as Schedule H, Schedule R, and Schedule MB. They are out of the intended scope of this analysis, despite all
schedules having their own analytical benefits.
